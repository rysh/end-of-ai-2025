Dear Dr. Venema,

We are witnessing a sustainability crisis in Large Language Models (LLMs). While the community focuses on "scaling laws," current architectures face a structural inevitability we term the "Logic Trap"—a convergence into closed loops of logical fallacies and ethical decay due to linear data accumulation.

I am submitting the enclosed manuscript to be considered for publication as a Perspective in Nature Machine Intelligence. In this work, I propose a fundamental paradigm shift: moving from "Static Accumulation" to "Dissipative Intelligence."

Crucially, this paper provides a mathematical proof linking the ethical failures of AI (sycophancy, hallucination) to the inability of linear stack approximations to model causal phase transitions. By introducing the Landau-Stuart equation from non-equilibrium thermodynamics into the domain of AI architecture, I demonstrate that "forgetting" (dissipation) is not a flaw, but a necessary condition for robust intelligence in dynamic environments.

This work integrates insights from:

1. AI Ethics: Defining the "Logic Trap" and "Self-Affirmation Trap" (experimentally reproduced in recent Zenodo preprints).
2. Nonlinear Physics: Applying bifurcation theory to causal confidence modeling.
3. Architecture: Proposing a bio-inspired "Predictive Inhibition" mechanism.

Given Nature Machine Intelligence's commitment to bridging machine learning with other scientific disciplines and addressing societal impact, I believe this physical-mathematical critique of current AI trends will be of significant interest to your broad readership, ranging from computer scientists to physicists and ethicists.

A preprint of this work has been deposited in Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18084010) to facilitate open scientific discourse.
